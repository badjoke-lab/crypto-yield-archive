# Phase 10 candidate review — HashKey Japan 貸暗号資産

Candidate target: HashKey Japan (formerly Tokyo Hash) 貸暗号資産.

## Reviewed authority

Current first-party HashKey Japan lending page describes a crypto-asset consumption-loan service in which customers lend crypto assets for a fixed period, receive the same type/equivalent quantity back at maturity, and receive contractual lending fees. The page also states that borrowed assets are operated within the parent HashKey Group.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent an exact original launch date unless a first-party dated launch authority is found.
- Published lending rates are issuer product terms, not independently verified realized returns.
- The statement that assets are operated within HashKey Group is an operator description, not independent proof of investment performance, solvency, reserves, segregation, insurance, or guaranteed repayment.
- Preserve Tokyo Hash / HashKey Japan naming continuity without assuming a legal-entity transition date that has not been directly verified.
- Current return mechanics do not establish universal historical repayment performance.

## Candidate gate

Pre-staging repository searches for `HashKey Japan`, `Tokyo Hash`, and the lending identity returned no indexed CYA PR identity. Hardened full-corpus scanning on the exact candidate PR head remains authoritative.
