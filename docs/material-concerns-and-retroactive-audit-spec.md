# CYA material concerns and retroactive audit specification

Status: canonical implementation specification
Parent issue: #323

## Purpose

CYA records the history and outcomes of crypto yield/lending products. Canonical inclusion, an active service state, or a verified source is not an endorsement, solvency finding, principal guarantee, or statement that advertised yield is sustainable or realized.

## Public material-concern summary

Every canonical platform detail page must support an evidence-backed summary covering, where material: principal guarantee or explicit lack of guarantee; material promotional or anomalous yield history; disclosed source-of-yield and limits of that disclosure; terms/ownership/segregation/priority risk; withdrawal restrictions or suspensions; regulatory actions; insolvency/failure-chain events; and customer outcome/recovery state.

The UI must distinguish confirmed facts, issuer/operator claims, allegations, unresolved unknowns and not-applicable fields. Advertised APR/APY is a historical product term, not verified realized return. CYA must not become a live APY ranking or a proprietary safety score.

## Retroactive audit

Apply this contract to every existing canonical platform/product. For each material dimension classify coverage as `derivable`, `research_required`, or `not_applicable`. Absence of evidence must not be converted into a favorable conclusion. Existing records that would be misleading under the new presentation must be corrected in reviewable batches.

## IZAKA-YA first application

IZAKA-YA is the first new intake governed by this specification. Record dated ordinary and promotional yield terms, including JPYR-linked promotions, together with principal-guarantee statements, disclosed yield-source explanations, withdrawal/service events, operator/jurisdiction and customer outcome only as supported by evidence.

Cross-link related JPYR (SOG), IZAKA-YA Wallet (WLR) and IZAKA-YA exchange/service (HEI) records when available. Each registry retains authority for its own classifications.

## Completion gate

Issue #323 is not complete until the relevant methodology/UI/roadmap documentation references this contract, all existing canonical platforms have been audited, required correction batches are merged, the public concern summary is implemented, IZAKA-YA is reviewed under the same rules, and repository validation/build/production parity gates pass.
