## Scope

This candidate is **PBR LENDING**, the crypto-asset lending service operated by Portobello Road株式会社.

## Current first-party basis

The current company page identifies Portobello Road株式会社 as operator and describes PBR LENDING as its crypto-asset lending business.

The current Terms define the service as crypto-asset consumption-loan transactions in which customers lend crypto assets to the company and the company owes return of the same asset plus contractual interest.

Individual lending contracts are formed after the customer funds the lending-preparation wallet, selects the plan / asset / quantity, submits a lending request, and the company processes the loan. Current Terms distinguish Regular Lending and Premium Lending with different lock periods and advertised rates.

Current risk / consent terms state that:

- the transaction is not a deposit or deposit-like product and is not covered by deposit insurance;
- the lending transaction is not crypto-asset exchange business and borrowed assets are outside statutory segregated-management treatment;
- no physical or personal collateral is provided;
- if Portobello Road enters insolvency proceedings, customer claims are general claims alongside other creditors;
- the company or its lending / investment counterparties can fail, making asset return difficult or impossible;
- liquidity, system and regulatory risks also apply.

The current asset-management terms say borrowed assets are separated operationally from company assets but do not represent legally complete trust protection.

First-party materials say borrowed crypto is used in crypto-market operations and/or business investments. That describes the operator's stated economic model; it is not independent verification of realized returns, reserves, investment performance or solvency.

## Evidence boundary

Advertised rates, security language, internal reserve / accumulation-fund claims, business-investment performance claims and repayment claims remain issuer statements unless independently corroborated. They must not be presented as guaranteed realized performance, reserve proof, insurance or solvency evidence.
