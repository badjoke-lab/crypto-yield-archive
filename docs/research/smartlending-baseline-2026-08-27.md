Source: https://smartlending.jp/faq/

## Product / rate baseline

The public service page advertises fixed 11% annual interest for the ordinary lending plan and 13% for the one-year plan across BTC, ETH, XRP, ADA, USDT, USDC, JPYC, DOT, SOL, TRX, SHIB, DOGE and POL. It says borrowed customer crypto is allocated across selected domestic and overseas funds and advertises a company-funded return-preparation asset pool, but the reviewed public page does not identify the funds or expose independently verifiable portfolio balances, realized profits, reconciliation or reserve proof.

Source: https://smartlending.jp/services/

## Contract structure and customer claim boundary

The terms dated 2026-08-20 define the product as crypto-asset consumption lending to SmartLending. The displayed "lending preparation wallet" is expressly described as a pseudo balance display rather than an actual wallet holding customer assets. The terms say assets loaned to the company are managed separately from company assets but are not guaranteed to be held in a legally complete trust. In company bankruptcy/reorganization, customer claims are stated to be ordinary unsecured claims ranking with other creditors.

The ordinary plan is 30 days at principle 11% annual interest and the one-year plan is 365 days at principle 13%. Early cancellation may incur a 20% deduction from the crypto to be returned. After a withdrawal request the terms allow up to seven business days for sending crypto and allow a withdrawal to be refused or held in specified circumstances, including failure to cooperate with verification and sudden social/economic changes or concentrated withdrawal requests.

The terms also permit the company, under specified unavoidable circumstances, to alter lending conditions or return value in Japanese yen instead of the original crypto asset.

Source: https://smartlending.jp/policies/terms-of-service/

## Public-copy / terms distinctions worth tracking

The marketing surface advertises return in roughly 2–3 business days, while the legal terms allow up to seven business days after a withdrawal request. This should be preserved as a terms-versus-marketing distinction, not automatically labeled a contradiction or failure.

The public service page says assets are diversified into selected domestic/overseas funds. The reviewed terms do not identify those funds. Treat the stated yield source and reserve/return-preparation claims as operator claims until independently verifiable deployment/reconciliation evidence exists.
