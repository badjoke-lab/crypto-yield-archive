Status: in progress

## Canonical structure finding

CYA stores historical event material across canonical event batches. The new concern summary must therefore be derived from dated event/evidence history rather than a live APY field or a new one-off IZAKA-YA warning object.

## Audit dimensions

Every canonical platform is to be classified for: principal guarantee; material yield promotions/anomalies; source-of-yield disclosure; terms/ownership/segregation/priority risk; withdrawal restriction/suspension; regulatory action; insolvency/failure chain; and customer outcome/recovery.

Each dimension is `derivable`, `research_required`, or `not_applicable`. Advertised yield is never equivalent to realized return. Missing evidence is never favorable evidence.

## Correction rule

Where historical event/evidence already establishes a material failure, restriction, regulatory action or customer outcome, the public summary must derive it without requiring a new canonical fact. Where the current event corpus cannot answer a material question, create research work rather than infer safety.

## IZAKA-YA boundary

IZAKA-YA is a first application of the same platform-wide contract. Its promotional yields, principal-guarantee statement, disclosed yield-source explanation, withdrawal/service history, operator/jurisdiction and customer outcome must be represented as dated claims/events with evidence strength preserved.
