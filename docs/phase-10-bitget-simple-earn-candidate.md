## Scope

This candidate is **Bitget Simple Earn / Savings** as a yield product family.

Do not treat every Bitget Earn product as economically identical. The initial canonical promotion should preserve the distinction between Simple Earn / Savings and separate structured products such as Shark Fin or Dual Investment unless product-level evidence supports a broader record boundary.

## Current first-party basis

The current Bitget Simple Earn / Savings surface is live and lists flexible and fixed crypto yield products across many assets.

Current FAQ material states that Flexible subscriptions deduct funds from the user's spot account, accrue hourly or daily interest, credit interest back to the spot account, and permit redemption under the applicable product terms.

The current Simple Earn Terms, last updated 2025-09-29, state that Bitget acts solely as platform provider in relation to Simple Earn while users' digital assets are managed by third parties. The terms say those third parties may or may not hold applicable licenses in the user's jurisdiction and warn that principal may not be recovered.

A first-party announcement dated 2022-08-29 proves that Bitget Earn Flexible Savings products were operating by that date.

Current and historical APRs, principal-guarantee marketing language, product-safety language, and third-party management descriptions remain issuer terms/claims. They are not independently verified realized returns, guarantees, reserve proof, insurance, or solvency evidence.

## Legal-entity boundary

The public product surface is global and Bitget services may be provided through different legal entities or jurisdictional arrangements. Do not infer one universal operating entity from the product page. Canonical promotion must preserve this uncertainty explicitly.
