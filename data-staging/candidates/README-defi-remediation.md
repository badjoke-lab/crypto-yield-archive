# DeFi remediation staging notes

Files in this remediation lane are research/staging artifacts only. They do not change canonical CYA data under `data/`.

New DeFi candidates must preserve an explicit architecture/type distinction and must not be rejected solely because they are non-custodial or protocol-based. Historically significant protocol-level lifecycle events are eligible for review; indiscriminate pool, deployment, TVL, APY, or market inventory is not.

Tectonic is the immediate time-sensitive review. Historical backfill runs in parallel and must not delay current incident capture.
