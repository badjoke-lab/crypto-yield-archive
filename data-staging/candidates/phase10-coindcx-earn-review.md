# Phase 10 candidate review — CoinDCX Earn

Candidate: `cya_candidate_000144`
Issue: #304

## First-party authority

- Current CoinDCX Earn help states users deploy supported virtual digital assets/tokens for potential returns over a selected duration and authorize CoinDCX to deploy those assets on their behalf.
- Current Earn terms disclose a minimum seven-day holding period, variable earning rates, possible zero earnings on early exit, CoinDCX discretion to modify/suspend/discontinue the product, and risk of partial or complete loss of deployed assets.
- Current support states deployed assets may be used across yield-generating opportunities including margin trading and staking.
- Legacy DCX Lend documentation establishes earlier crypto-lending functionality with selected terms and locked lending rates.

## Boundaries

- Do not treat displayed rates as guaranteed or realized returns.
- Do not infer one unchanged custody/ownership/legal model across legacy DCX Lend and current Earn.
- Do not infer an original Earn launch date from help-center timestamps.
- Current operation is supported by current first-party help surfaces; legal-entity and jurisdiction boundaries remain product-specific.
