## First-party authority

- Current ZebPay Earn FAQ describes fixed-term crypto deposits with 30/60/90-day tenures.
- At maturity, invested crypto plus earnings are credited to the wallet under the product terms.
- Early exit is permitted without principal penalty, but the user receives no earnings for the exited term.
- Current ZebPay Earn product and account/help surfaces still expose Earn as an active crypto action.
- The FAQ creation timestamp is 2021-03-15, but this is not treated as an exact product launch date without separate launch authority.

## Boundaries

- Advertised annualized earning percentages are issuer terms, not verified realized returns or guarantees.
- Do not infer deposit insurance, segregation, insolvency priority or universal historical repayment.
- Exact original launch date remains unresolved.
