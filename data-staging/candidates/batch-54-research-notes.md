## Bitstamp Earn Staking

Reviewed first-party basis:

- Bitstamp announced on 13 July 2021 that Ethereum staking was live under Bitstamp Earn, after an April 2021 early-access rollout;
- the launch material describes staking ETH held in a Bitstamp account and Bitstamp handling the technical staking process on the customer's behalf;
- Bitstamp later added ALGO rewards under Bitstamp Earn and subsequently wound ALGO staking down on 1 April 2025 while leaving other Earn/staking products separate;
- the current Bitstamp Learn surface continues to expose Bitstamp Earn and describes staking as an available yield function, while current ETH educational material still points users to staking ETH at Bitstamp;
- product availability is jurisdiction-dependent and Bitstamp's own educational material states staking is unavailable in some jurisdictions;
- CYA must keep ETH staking, ALGO staking history, and any lending products product-scoped rather than treating every Bitstamp Earn mechanism as one identical yield source or legal arrangement.

- https://blog.bitstamp.net/post/eth-staking-coming-bitstamp-sign-early-access/
- https://blog.bitstamp.net/post/winding-down-algo-staking-on-bitstamp/
- https://www.bitstamp.net/learn/

## Crypto.com On-Chain Staking

Reviewed first-party basis:

- Crypto.com's current Staking surface documents active on-chain staking across multiple proof-of-stake assets;
- the current help material states that rewards are generated by the relevant blockchain protocol, that Crypto.com deducts service fees, and that no particular rate of return is guaranteed;
- current first-party material documents bonding/unbonding behavior, validator/network timing, protocol-specific reward cycles and the possibility that users do not receive rewards when unstaking early or during protocol unbonding periods;
- Crypto.com states that it maintains separate blockchain addresses and wallets for facilitating users' on-chain staking assets;
- supported assets, estimated rates, activation timing and unbonding periods are product/network-specific and change over time;
- the reviewed current materials establish an active staking service but do not, by themselves, establish one exact original umbrella launch date for the present multi-asset On-Chain Staking product.

Primary sources:

- https://crypto.com/en/staking
- https://help.crypto.com/en/articles/7886980-staking-crypto-on-crypto-com-app
- https://help.crypto.com/en/articles/6800043-on-chain-staking-guide

Decision: `add_now`, subject to hardened duplicate scanning, corpus audit and review-only draft generation. If duplicate-clear, keep `launch_date` unresolved unless an explicit first-party original-launch source is found during canonical review. Do not infer legal title, insolvency protection or universal segregation from operational wallet-address language; preserve it only as an attributed product statement.
